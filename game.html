Embrace the darkness, become the legend

In a city consumed by chaos and darkness, an ancient evil has awakened. As the legendary Abyss Wolf, you must traverse through corrupted landscapes, face creatures of doom, and harness your growing powers to save the city from their destructive wrath.

Warning:

Game contains intense action sequences, dark themes, and atmospheric horror elements. Player discretion is advised.

  • Multiple endings based on your choices and discoveries
